# Code Of Conduct

Agentic Sim2Real should be a respectful, practical place to collaborate on safety-sensitive robotics tooling.

## Expected Behavior

- Be direct, kind, and specific in technical feedback.
- Assume good intent while still naming risks clearly.
- Keep discussions focused on evidence, reproducibility, and user impact.
- Respect privacy around customer data, robot logs, calibration files, and credentials.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private data, secrets, or safety-sensitive details without permission.
- Pressuring maintainers or contributors to bypass review, tests, or human hardware gates.
- Repeatedly derailing technical discussion after maintainers ask to refocus.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or block participation when behavior puts people, data, or hardware safety at risk.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Add a dedicated contact here before publishing the repository publicly: `[maintainer-contact@example.com]`.
